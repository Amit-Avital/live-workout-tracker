Live Workout Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Live Workout Tracker is an iPhone and Apple Watch app operated by Amit Avital. This policy explains how the app and this website handle information. For privacy questions or support, email lp4company@gmail.com.

1. Information on your devices

The app stores your exercise library, routines, exercise notes, sets, weights, workout dates and durations, completed workout history, and preferences on your devices. It also keeps temporary session and recovery information so an interrupted workout can be recovered.

This information is used to run workouts, display your progress, remember your choices, and recover interrupted sessions. The app does not require an account and does not send your workout records to the developer. There is no developer-operated workout server, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK in the app.

2. Apple Health

With your permission, the app can read heart rate and active energy from Apple Health and write workouts and active energy to Apple Health. These permissions support live metrics during Apple Watch workouts and saving completed Watch workouts. Starting a workout from iPhone on your Watch can also request Health permission.

Workouts recorded only on iPhone are not currently saved to Apple Health. The app’s own workout history does not store raw heart-rate samples. A saved Watch workout may have an Apple Health workout identifier associated with its app history record.

Health information is used for your workout features, not for advertising, marketing, sale, or data brokerage. You can review and change the app’s Health permissions in Apple Health or your device’s privacy settings. Denying or withdrawing access can limit Health metrics or workout saving. Withdrawing permission does not automatically delete records already saved.

3. Your paired iPhone and Apple Watch

The app uses Apple’s paired-device services for a limited set of transfers:

  • Your exercise library, routines, and preferences are sent from iPhone to Apple Watch.
  • Completed workout history is sent from Apple Watch to iPhone. Pending deliveries are kept locally and retried until acknowledged.
  • A device can make a one-time check of whether the other device already has an active workout.
  • iPhone can request that the Watch app launch and start a workout.

Each active workout runs independently on the device where it starts. Active workout snapshots, live timers, workout controls, and live health metrics are not mirrored between devices. These paired-device transfers do not send workout data to the developer.

4. Notifications

The app schedules local rest-completion notifications and provides workout sounds and haptics on the device running the workout. Local notification information can include an identifier used to reopen the matching workout. These alerts do not use a developer-operated push-notification service. You can change notification permissions in your iPhone or Apple Watch notification settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Saved routines, exercise information, preferences, and history remain in local app storage until you remove them using the available controls or delete the app’s data. Recovery information is maintained as needed for session recovery and cleanup; pending completed-history deliveries remain until acknowledged.

  • Routines and history: you can delete routines and completed workouts using their deletion controls in the iPhone app.
  • Exercises: archiving an exercise moves it into Deleted Exercises and preserves its information for restoration. Permanent deletion removes the exercise, its remembered weights and set information, and matching completed sets. Workouts left with no sets are removed. A minimal exercise identifier is retained to prevent deleted information from being restored by later transfers or built-in library setup.
  • Separate copies: deleting a workout from app history does not delete its Apple Health record and does not necessarily remove copies on the other device. Manage Apple Health records separately in the Health app.
  • Removing local app storage: delete the app from each device whose local data you want to remove. Offloading the iPhone app retains its data. Removing the app does not by itself delete Apple Health records or existing backups.

The app does not operate its own cloud backup or CloudKit synchronization service. Apple-managed device backups and Apple Health synchronization may retain or restore information according to your Apple settings. You can manage those copies through Apple’s controls. See Apple’s Health privacy information.

The developer cannot access or remotely delete workout data stored only on your devices. Contact us if you need help finding the relevant deletion controls.

6. Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Our email provider, Google’s Gmail service, processes this correspondence to deliver and store it. We use it to respond to your request and provide support. Please include only information needed for your question.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle your request, related follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing lp4company@gmail.com. See Google’s Privacy Policy for its handling of email services.

7. This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, contact forms, cookies, or third-party font services. Opening the privacy policy in your browser is a website visit; the app does not attach your workout data to the link.

GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. GitHub handles hosting information under its Privacy Statement. You can also read GitHub’s explanation of Pages data collection.

8. Policy changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when our privacy practices change. Previous versions are retained in the website’s public revision history.

For questions about this policy or requests concerning support correspondence, contact Amit Avital at lp4company@gmail.com.
